The FreeRTOS project can be contacted by emailing Richard Barry - r.barry @ FreeRTOS.org (remove the 
spaces before and after the @ for the valid address).

Please note that support requests should **not** be sent to this email address unless the sender has 
a valid support contract. Users wishing to obtain free support are requested to make use of 
the [FreeRTOS monitored support forum](http://sourceforge.net/projects/freertos/forums/forum/382005) - 
which is specifically provided for this purpose.
