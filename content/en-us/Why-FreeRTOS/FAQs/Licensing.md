## Can I use FreeRTOS in a commercial project?

Yes.   
Please read the [software license](/Documentation/02-Kernel/01-About-the-FreeRTOS-kernel/04-Licensing) information page for a full answer.


## Do I have to give away my application source code if I use FreeRTOS?

No.   
Please read the [software license](/Documentation/02-Kernel/01-About-the-FreeRTOS-kernel/04-Licensing) information page for a full answer.


## I require a commercial license, is there anything available?

Yes.   
OPENRTOS from WITTENSTEIN high integrity systems provides a commercial license for FreeRTOS. More information 
at [highintegritysystems.com/openrtos](https://www.highintegritysystems.com/openrtos/).


## Can I use FreeRTOS in a safety critical application?

SAFERTOS, from WITTENSTEIN high integrity systems, shares the same functional model as FreeRTOS and is 
certified to safety standards. More information 
at [highintegritysystems.com/openrtos](https://www.highintegritysystems.com/openrtos/)
