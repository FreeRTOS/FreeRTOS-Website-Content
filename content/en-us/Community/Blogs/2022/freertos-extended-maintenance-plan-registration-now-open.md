We are excited to announce that registration for the FreeRTOS Extended Maintenance Plan (EMP) is now 
open. FreeRTOS EMP subscriptions allow embedded developers to receive critical bug fixes and security 
patches on their chosen FreeRTOS Long Term Support (LTS) version for up to 10 years beyond the end of 
its initial [support period](/Community/Blogs/2021/freertos-aws-reference-integrations-now-include-freertos-202012-01-lts-libraries). During the subscription period, developers will 
receive notifications for upcoming patches on FreeRTOS libraries, allowing them to systematically plan 
their product maintenance activities. This helps developers to secure their microcontroller-based 
devices for years, save operating system upgrade costs, and reduce the risks associated with patching 
their devices. 

To learn more, refer to 
the [FreeRTOS features](https://aws.amazon.com/freertos/features/), [pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/freertos/pricing/), 
and [frequently asked questions](https://aws.amazon.com/freertos/faqs/) webpages on AWS. To get started, 
see the [FreeRTOS Getting Started Guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/freertos/latest/userguide/freertos-getting-started-emp.html) 
for EMP.
