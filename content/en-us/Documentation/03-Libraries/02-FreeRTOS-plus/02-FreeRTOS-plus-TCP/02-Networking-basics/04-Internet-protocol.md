[Internet Protocol](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IP_network) is the "IP" in [TCP/IP](/Documentation/03-Libraries/02-FreeRTOS-plus/02-FreeRTOS-plus-TCP/01-FreeRTOS-Plus-TCP) and [UDP/IP](/Documentation/03-Libraries/02-FreeRTOS-plus/02-FreeRTOS-plus-TCP/02-Networking-basics/09-UDP).

The Internet Protocol suite are the protocols used to communicate across networks (inter-networking), such
as on the Internet. Nodes on an IP network are identified by their [IP address](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IP_address).

IP packets can be carried in, amongst many other things, [Ethernet frames](/Documentation/03-Libraries/02-FreeRTOS-plus/02-FreeRTOS-plus-TCP/02-Networking-basics/02-Ethernet-addressing),
and can themselves carry [TCP](/Documentation/03-Libraries/02-FreeRTOS-plus/02-FreeRTOS-plus-TCP/01-FreeRTOS-Plus-TCP) and [UDP](/Documentation/03-Libraries/02-FreeRTOS-plus/02-FreeRTOS-plus-TCP/02-Networking-basics/09-UDP) data.
