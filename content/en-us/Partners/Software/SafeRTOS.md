## SAFERTOS or FreeRTOS?

SAFE**RTOS** is based on the functional model of the FreeRTOS kernel,
however, it is not the FreeRTOS kernel. It has been completely
re-designed by a team of functional safety experts at WHIS. Their
engineers took the FreeRTOS kernel functional model, subjected it to a
full HAZOP, and generated a set of safety requirements. The resulting
functional and safety requirements sets were put through an IEC 61508-3
SIL 3 development life cycle, creating the SAFE**RTOS** code base and DAP.
 
Free demos are available for SAFE**RTOS** : [Download them here](https://www.highintegritysystems.com/down-loads/manuals-datasheets/safertos-datasheet-downloads/).
 

## Easier Certification

Certifying SAFE**RTOS** integrated within a product is a simple and hassle-free process.
 
Contained within the Design Assurance Pack, or DAP, (which is included
with SAFE**RTOS** purchases) is the all-important Safety Manual. The Safety
Manual explains exactly how to install and integrate SAFE**RTOS** into your
development environment. Following the concise instructions will also
generate the evidence required by your auditors to confirm the process
has been followed correctly. This removes the need for re-testing on
your target hardware, and provides a solid, dependable platform for your
development. The DAP contains all the Planning, Design and Verification
artefacts, and is specific to the selected Processor/Compiler combination.

Available pre-certified to IEC 61508 SIL3 by TÜV SÜD in 2007, SAFE**RTOS** is ideal for industrial

WHIS has created a complete RTOS package for the Automotive sector:

* SAFECheckpoints - fulfils the requirement of ISO 26262 ASIL C&D software designs to have a runtime monitor
