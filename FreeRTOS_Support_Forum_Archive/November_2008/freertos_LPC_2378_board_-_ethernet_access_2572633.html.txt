</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">LPC 2378 board &#8211; ethernet access</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/pfav63">pfav63</a> on November 20, 2008</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hi Spreet,
let us to be focused on Ethernet LEDs
on Keil board there are 3 eth. LEDs: 2 on RJ connectors and one near the DP83848 &#40;eth PHY&#41;.
The led on the board indicate the link speed &#40;on = 100Mb&#41;
The two on RJ connector indicate LINK and Activity.
When link is ok LINK led is always ON and if the link is 100Mb the led on the board is on too.
Activity led should blink. 
So if on RJ connector you don&#8217;t have a led always on this means ethernet link is down&#33;&#33;&#33;
On this case you have to investigate why link is down.
In the past I used this eval-board for a while and the FreeRTOS demo compiled by GNU tools was working.
let me know
Paolo

</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">LPC 2378 board &#8211; ethernet access</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/spreetsingh">spreetsingh</a> on November 20, 2008</div><div class="thread-post-content">I have finally gotten it to work. Issue is with the delays provided in the initialization of the EMAC. I had to go and increase the delay in the auto negotiation of the speed of connection &#8211; line number 184 of emac.c. By increasing the delay from 10 to 100 in VTaskdelay&#40;100&#41; &#8211; it seems to work for now.
