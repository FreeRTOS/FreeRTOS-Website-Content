</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">HCS12X port and portENABLE_INTERRUPTS()</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on May 24, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">As far as I recall, the HCS12 port does not support nested interrupts.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">HCS12X port and portENABLE_INTERRUPTS()</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/xz8987f">xz8987f</a> on May 24, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">HCS12 only has one interrupt inhibit bit, so no nested interrupts. You &#42;could&#42; re-enable interrupts in the ISR, but this will enable &#42;all&#42; interrupts. To the extend that even a lower priority interrupt can occur. That&#8217;s usually not what you want.<br />
So indeed: nested interrupts for HCS12 is not supported &#40;by the hardware, and by the port&#41;.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">HCS12X port and portENABLE_INTERRUPTS()</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/ssn1971">ssn1971</a> on May 24, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">Sory,<br />
