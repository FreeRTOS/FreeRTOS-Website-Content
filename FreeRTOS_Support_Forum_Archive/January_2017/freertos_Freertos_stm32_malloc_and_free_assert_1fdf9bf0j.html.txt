</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Freertos stm32 : malloc and free assert</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/rtel">rtel</a> on January 24, 2017</div><div class="thread-post-content">Which asserts are being hit? returned from pvPortMalloc() before you use it?
