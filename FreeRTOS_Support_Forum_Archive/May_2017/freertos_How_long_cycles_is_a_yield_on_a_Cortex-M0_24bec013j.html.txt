  <main class="main-content"><div class="thread-post"><h2 class="thread-post-title">How long (cycles) is a yield on a Cortex-M0+?</h2><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/ljskinny">ljskinny</a> on May 11, 2017</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hi all,
We have a strange problem in using a time critial interrupt (samplin input) in conjunction with the freeRTOS.
my first question is, how long a yield is, which uses the critical section and disables the global interrupts.
So this could be the reason for blocking the interrupt. 
I would expect, that the yield should not block that long the interupt or i am i something missing?
Any ideas are welcome. I have to check, if the RTOS itself is the problem for the issues in our application.
Best regards
Holle
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">How long (cycles) is a yield on a Cortex-M0+?</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/rtel">rtel</a> on May 11, 2017</div><div class="thread-post-content">If you are referring to the context switch time then you can find some 
information for a Cortex-M3 on the link below.  An M0 will be a bit 
slower due to its more limited instruction set.
Also unlike an M3, the M0 will globally disable interrupts in a critical 
section &#8211; do you have long critical sections? 
(taskENTER<em>CRITICAL()/taskEXIT</em>CRITICAL)
