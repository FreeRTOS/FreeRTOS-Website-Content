Any suggestions ?
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">MPU on cortex-m3</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/michaldemin">michaldemin</a> on November 30, 2009</div><div class="thread-post-content">I forgot that im using STM32  
Anyone knows how is the stm32 behaving when DMA transfer is in progress and i change the content of MPU ?  
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">MPU on cortex-m3</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on November 30, 2009</div><div class="thread-post-content">First, are you sure your STM32 has an MPU?  
&#42;If&#42; it does, then I would expect the NVIC registers will be protected.&nbsp; There are a few that you can access without a fault being generated, but without the CPU taking notice of the read or write.  
If you want to access the registers then you need to do it from privileged code, this is in the end of the point of the MPU, to protect critical data.&nbsp; You can either setup the NVIC from main&#40;&#41; before the scheduler is started, or do it from a privileged task, or do it from a task that is created privileged to allow it to perform this type of setup before being set to User mode to carry out its main function.  
Regards.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">MPU on cortex-m3</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/michaldemin">michaldemin</a> on November 30, 2009</div><div class="thread-post-content">Thanks for reply.  
I didn&#8217;t notice that MPU is &#95;optional&#95; for the Cortex-M3. So that&#8217;s where my problem was.  
&#8220;Type&#8221; register check from GDB confirms my mistake:  
print/x &#42;0xe000ed90<br />
$2 = 0x0  
Thanks a lot&nbsp; :&#41;
