</pre>
where GDT_BYTES is:
<pre>
#define GDT_ENTRIES 8
#define GDT_ENTRY_SIZE  8
#define GDT_BYTES   (GDT_ENTRIES * GDT_ENTRY_SIZE)
</pre>
so at a guess you could try modifying the line to hard code the 
GDT_BYTES value as such:
<pre>
gdt: .space 64
</pre>
