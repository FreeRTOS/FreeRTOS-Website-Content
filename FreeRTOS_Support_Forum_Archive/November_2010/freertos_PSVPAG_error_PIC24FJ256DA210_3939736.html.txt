</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">PSVPAG error PIC24FJ256DA210</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/woops_">woops_</a> on November 10, 2010</div><div class="thread-post-content">The MicrochipMPLAB C30supportPIC24Fh folder holds a header for each device individually in which PSVPAG is referenced. Are you including the header? It is normally inc from FreeRTOSconfig header.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">PSVPAG error PIC24FJ256DA210</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richard_damon">richard_damon</a> on November 10, 2010</div><div class="thread-post-content">It looks like that chip has a new feature &#40;Extended Data Space&#41; that may require a small change in the port to handle. It looks like the PSVPAG register becomes the DSWPAG and DSRPAG for EDS parts.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">PSVPAG error PIC24FJ256DA210</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/*anonymous">*anonymous</a> on August 5, 2011</div><div class="thread-post-content">A late reply, but here goes.  
I had the same problem with a EDS pic device, a PIC24FJ256GB210.<br />
I found a post on the microchip forum that dealt with the same issue: <a href="http://www.microchip.com/forums/m568144-print.aspx">http://www.microchip.com/forums/m568144-print.aspx</a>.  
The suggested solution is to change the kernel code in a few places. In port.c under the portRESTORE&#95;CONTEXT&#40;&#41; macro, replace the line &#8220;POP PSVPAG&#8221; with POP &#8220;DSWPAG&#8221; and &#8220;POP DSRPAG&#8221;. The order of the pops is important&#33;  
In port.c under in the pxPortInitialiseStack function definition, replace the lines 
<pre><code>*pxTopOfStack = PSVPAG; pxTopOfStack++;
</code></pre>
with 
<pre><code>*pxTopOfStack = DSRPAG; pxTopOfStack++; *pxTopOfStack = DSWPAG; pxTopOfStack++
</code></pre>
.  
Finally in portasm&#95;PIC24.S in the &#95;vPortYield section, replace &#8220;PUSH PSVPAG&#8221; with &#8220;PUSH DSRPAG&#8221; and &#8220;PUSH DSWPAG&#8221;, and replace the line &#8220;POP PSVPAG&#8221; with &#8220;POP DSWPAG&#8221; and &#8220;POP DSRPAG&#8221;. Again, notice the order of push and pops.  
These changes make FreeRTOS run on the fore mentioned micro, but I cannot guarantee that they will work under all circumstances.
