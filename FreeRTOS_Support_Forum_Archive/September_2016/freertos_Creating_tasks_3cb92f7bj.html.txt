</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Creating tasks</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/rtel">rtel</a> on September 28, 2016</div><div class="thread-post-content">If configTICK<em>RATE</em>HZ is 1000, then the tick frequency will be 
1000Hz/1KHz.  So one tick every ms, and a timing granularity of 1ms (so 
specifying a 1ms delay will give you between 0.0001ms and 0.9999ms). 
You cannot specify a 0.5ms delay time with a 1KHz tick rate, and it is 
not recommended to go over 1KHz.  You can however trigger tasks from an 
interrupt, and a link to the recommended way of doing that has already 
been provided.
Normally you would use the pdMS<em>TO</em>TICKS() macro to convert milliseconds 
into tick periods, but that won&#8217;t work if the tick rate is greater than 
1KHz.
