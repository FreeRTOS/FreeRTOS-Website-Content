&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; pxTopOfStack&#8211;;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &#42;pxTopOfStack = &#40; portSTACK&#95;TYPE &#41; 0x12121212;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; /&#42; R12 &#42;/

</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">task ending</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on August 24, 2006</div><div class="thread-post-content">My preference was to structure tasks as:
void vATask&#40; void &#42;pvParameters &#41;
&#123;
&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;loop
&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#123;
&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;do task stuff here
&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;&#125;
&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;/&#42; We broke out of the loop. &#42;/
&#95;&#95;&#95;&#95;vTaskDelete&#40; NULL &#41;;
