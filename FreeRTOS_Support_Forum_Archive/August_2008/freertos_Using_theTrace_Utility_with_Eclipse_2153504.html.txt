</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Using theTrace Utility with Eclipse</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/incrediball">incrediball</a> on August 7, 2008</div><div class="thread-post-content">I thought you were talking about one of the tools with Eclipse or something that came with the Amontec package.
