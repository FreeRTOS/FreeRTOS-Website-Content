  <main class="main-content"><div class="thread-post"><h2 class="thread-post-title">running FreeRTOS on s3c2440A booting from nan</h2><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/cynthiya">cynthiya</a> on September 29, 2010</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hi   
I am trying to run FreeRTOS on s3c2440 ARM920 based SoC.  
The development board I am using doesn&#8217;t have a NOR flash to boot from. or any way to change the address mapping.<br />
the chip has an internal RAM called stepping stone which loads first 4K of NAND on reset.<br />
The rest of the RAM starts on this board at 0x30000000.<br />
I am using IAR development environment and i am able to run my application/OS by downloading it<br />
to RAM.. ie 0x00 to 4K ,then continuing at 0x30000000 till 0x38000000.&#40;64MB&#41;  
What I don&#8217;t know is how to start the Application /OS from NAND. the options i can think of are<br />
1.write a loader program at first 4k of NAND ,which is loaded in to stepping stone automatically in reset<br />
and the loader will then copy the application/OS to the RAM.<br />
&nbsp;&nbsp; but in this case how can I configure the exception vectors lying in the internal RAM/steppingstone once<br />
&nbsp; the system starts on the OS/application image in the external RAM.<br />
2.use no loading program.make and burn the application/os image directly on to the beginning of the NAND.<br />
on reset the the system starts running the first 4K copied in to the internal RAM.code in the first 4K will copy the rest of the<br />
image to external RAM .<br />
this seems the solution, but can someone familiar with IAR development environment tell me how to do this.  
i thought of doing something with MMU, but i think i am making things more complex.<br />
Appreciate any answer
