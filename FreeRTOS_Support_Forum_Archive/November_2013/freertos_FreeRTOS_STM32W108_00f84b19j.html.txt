</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">FreeRTOS STM32W108</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on November 5, 2013</div><div class="thread-post-content">I&#8217;ve had a quick look at the spec on the web, and it is &#8216;just&#8217; a Cortex-M3, and FreeRTOS will run on any Cortex-M3 chip.
The limitation in this case is the amount of RAM available, which is stated as &#8220;8/12/16 Kbytes of integrated RAM&#8221;.  That will limit the number of tasks you can create, as each task requires its own stack &#8211; which consumes a minimum of 70 words per task &#8211; much more if you the task is doing any serious work.
Regards.
