  <main class="main-content"><div class="thread-post"><h2 class="thread-post-title">portTICK_RATE_MS Resolution</h2><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/groufosse">groufosse</a> on December 28, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hi,<br />
In the &#8220;FreeRTOSConfig.h&#8221; file, this is declared:<br />
&#35;define configTICK&#95;RATE&#95;HZ &#40; &#40; portTickType &#41; 1000 &#41;  
In my program, I have this declared:<br />
&#35;define mainLED&#95;TOGGLE&#95;RATE &#40; &#40; portTickType &#41; 5000 / portTICK&#95;RATE&#95;MS &#41;  
When I use this:<br />
vTaskDelayUntil&#40; &amp;xLastFlashTime, mainLED&#95;TOGGLE&#95;RATE &#41;;  
I would expect it to flash every 5 seconds; however, it&#8217;s flashing at about 7 or 8 seconds. After reading the application manual, I think I understand this topic, but clearly I do not. Can anyone point out the error of my ways?  
Regards,<br />
Gary  
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">portTICK_RATE_MS Resolution</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on December 28, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">I suspect there will be a mismatch between the configCPU&#95;CLOCK&#95;HZ setting and the actual clock frequency being used.  
Regards.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">portTICK_RATE_MS Resolution</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/groufosse">groufosse</a> on December 28, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">That was exactly the problem; I displayed all the clock and peripheral registers and updated the value of configCPU&#95;CLOCK&#95;HZ to reflect that value. It&#8217;s now working fine.  
Thanks for the &#8220;timely&#8221; help&#33;  
Gary
