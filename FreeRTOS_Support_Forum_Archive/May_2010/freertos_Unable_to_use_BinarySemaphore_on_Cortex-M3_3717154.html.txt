</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Unable to use BinarySemaphore on Cortex-M3</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richard_damon">richard_damon</a> on May 21, 2010</div><div class="thread-post-content">The issue with I high priority interrupt accessing FreeRTOS structures is that they may not be in a stable condition, and accessing them may corrupt their state. FreeRTOS may &#40;depending on the port&#41; only block interrupts below a given priority when modifying critical resources, in order to not impact the latency of those higher priority interrupts that don&#8217;t need to interact with FreeRTOS.
