  <main class="main-content"><div class="thread-post"><h2 class="thread-post-title">Compilation warning on AVR GCC</h2><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/bmarques">bmarques</a> on February 5, 2010</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hello,  
I&#8217;m using FreeRTOS on a board I made with an AT90USB647 and the compiler issues a warning on line 422 of tasks.c:  
<em>FreeRTOS/tasks.c: In function &#8216;xTaskGenericCreate&#8217;:<br />
FreeRTOS/tasks.c:422: error: cast from pointer to integer of different size<br />
FreeRTOS/tasks.c:422: error: cast to pointer from integer of different size</em>  
in line 422 of tasks.c i can see:<br />
_pxTopOfStack = &#40; portSTACK&#95;TYPE &#42; &#41; &#40; &#40; &#40; unsigned long &#41; pxTopOfStack &#41; &amp; &#40; &#40; unsigned long &#41; ~portBYTE&#95;ALIGNMENT&#95;MASK&nbsp; &#41; &#41;;<br />
_  
in portmacro.h &#40;based on portmacro.h in the ATMega323 example&#41; i have:<br />
<em>&#35;define portSTACK&#95;TYPE unsigned portCHAR</em>  
is this the correct <em>portSTACK&#95;TYPE</em> for the AVR uController?<br />
What should i do? change portSTACK&#95;TYPE or change line 422 of tasks.c?<br />
If i replace &#8216;long&#8217; by &#8216;int&#8217; in line 422 the warning disapears and FreeRTOS works but is this correct?  
Regards,<br />
Bernardo Marques.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Compilation warning on AVR GCC</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on February 5, 2010</div><div class="thread-post-content">This warning has arrisen from a recent change and occurs on ports where the stack type is smaller than the pointer type.&nbsp; I think changing the unsigned long casts to portBASE&#95;TYPE cases might be ok, but would have to check each port individually.  
Regards.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Compilation warning on AVR GCC</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/bmarques">bmarques</a> on February 9, 2010</div><div class="thread-post-content">Nope. It cannot be a cast to <em>portBASE&#95;TYPE</em>, it will make it worse. I suppose that in AVR GCC a pointer has two bytes and the base type on the AVR is 8-bit. As <em>pxTopOfStack</em> must be casted to a 16-bit integer it must be to either <em>int</em> or <em>portSHORT</em>.
