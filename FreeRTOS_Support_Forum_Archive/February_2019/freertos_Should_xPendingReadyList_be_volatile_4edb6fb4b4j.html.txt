It is currently
ListItem<em>t * configLIST</em>VOLATILE pxIndex;
try
configLIST<em>VOLATILE ListItem</em>t *  pxIndex; 
(type * volatile ptr) means the thing at the pointer address is volatile
(volatile type * ptr) means the pointer itself is volatile
The compiler is correctly asssuming the pointer doesn&#8217;t change because it isn&#8217;t volatile the thing at the pointer is volatile .. it is behaving exactly as it should.
If you ever get confused go for safety
(volatile type * volatile ptr)
Now the pointer and what it points to are both volatile 🙂
