</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">data abort in heap_4.c</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/rtel">rtel</a> on February 10, 2014</div><div class="thread-post-content">heap<em>4.c has been thoroughly tested, both systematically and by thrashing.  Although obviously we can&#8217;t guarantee there aren&#8217;t any problems with heap</em>4 it is most likely that your problem lies elsewhere in your system.  lwIP being the most obvious place, but also it could simply be a configuration problem.
Which port of FreeRTOS are you using?  Is it up to date (newer version have more configASSERT() statements to trap common misconfigurations).  Do you have configASSERT() defined?  Do you have a stack overflow hook defined?  Have you looked through the &#8220;my application does not run what could be wrong?&#8221; page of the FAQ?
