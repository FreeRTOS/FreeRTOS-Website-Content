</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Scheduling question</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on January 9, 2006</div><div class="thread-post-content">Assuming you are using the preemptive scheduler:
T1 is the highest priority task.&nbsp; Any time T1 is not blocked it will execute.
T2 and T3 have the same priority, but a priority less than T1.&nbsp; T2 and T3 will only execute if T1 is blocked.&nbsp; When T1 is blocked T2 and T3 will take it in turns to execute.
Regards.
