</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">stats</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/jrfarmer">jrfarmer</a> on December 13, 2013</div><div class="thread-post-content">My guess is that your 32-bit timebase for the stats has wrapped.  When the timebase wraps, the stats become invalid.
What is your timebase for the stats?  Based on your description, I&#8217;m guessing you have a 10kHz (or 0.1msec tick) timebase for the stats calculation.  Consider (0xFFFFFFFF * 0.0001) ~= 5 days, so the timebase will wrap after 5 days.
