</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">CortexM3 HardFault with FreeRTOS7.5.2</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/rtel">rtel</a> on December 6, 2013</div><div class="thread-post-content">If the dump was done while you were in the hardfault handler then I would expect the PC to point to code inside the hardfault handler.
