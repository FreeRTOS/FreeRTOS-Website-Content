</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">stm32f107 startScheduler hang due to WWDG_IRQ </h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/davedoors">davedoors</a> on June 26, 2014</div><div class="thread-post-content">If the watchdog is enabled, which it is likely to be by default, and the interrupt is coming from the watchdog, which it would seem to be, then your problem is unlikely to be related to FreeRTOS &#8211; so try one step at a time.
Here is a verbose description of what you are going to have to do, note FreeRTOS is not mentioned in these instructions:
1) Get the user manual for the STM32 device you are using.
2) Look up the watchdog chapter in the manual.
3) In the watchdog chapter find the address of the register that enables and disables the watchdog interrupt.
4) In your debugger, look at the register and see if the watchdog is enabled or not.
5) If the watchdog is enabled then in main() write to the register to disable it.  Or
6) If the watchdog is not enabled continue to debug to find out what the problem is.
