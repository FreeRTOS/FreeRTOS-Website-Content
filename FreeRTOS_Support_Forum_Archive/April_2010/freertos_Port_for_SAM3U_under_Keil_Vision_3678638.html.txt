</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Port for SAM3U under Keil µVision</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/davedoors">davedoors</a> on April 16, 2010</div><div class="thread-post-content">That is because you are trying to use an ARM7 file &#40;which does support ARM code&#41; with a cortex M3 &#40;which does not support ARM code&#41;.  
You want to use the files from the RVDSARM&#95;CM3 directory.
