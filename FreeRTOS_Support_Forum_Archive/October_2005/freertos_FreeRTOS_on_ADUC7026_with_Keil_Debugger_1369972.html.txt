</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">FreeRTOS on ADUC7026 with Keil Debugger</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on October 18, 2005</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hi,
A couple of questions:
1&#41; How did you create the Keil project.&nbsp; Have you based it on the existing Keil LPC project?
2&#41; Are you using ARM or THUMB mode?&nbsp; The existing Keil project allows you to switch between the two depending on the KEIL&#95;THUMB&#95;INTERWORK definition &#40;amongst others&#41;.&nbsp; How portDISABLE&#95;INTERRUPTS&#40;&#41; works is very different between the ARM and THUMB builds.
