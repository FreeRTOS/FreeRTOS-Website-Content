</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">LPC21xx to ADUC7026 how to setup Interups</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/nobody">nobody</a> on October 20, 2005</div><div class="thread-post-content">I have never used an ADUC7026, but from a quick glance at the rather minimal datasheet &#40;link in previous post&#41;, it appears that there is a very simple interrupt system.&nbsp; When you get an IRQ interrupt you just have to look at the interrupt bit mask to see the origin of the interrupt and branch to the correct handler.&nbsp; There must be example on the AD WEB site of efficient ways of doing this?
As far as FreeRTOS goes there is not going to be much difference between this type of interrupt and an automatically vectored interrupt once you are in the handler itself.&nbsp; The only difference is how you get to the handler.
I should try getting the periodic interrupt working first in the absence of FreeRTOS &#40;the datasheet gives info on how to do this&#41;.&nbsp; Then just hook up the RTOS tick routine to the interrupt once you know it is working correctly.&nbsp; The syntax of how to write the routine will be the same as per the LPC Keil demo.&nbsp; Just make sure that the method of jumping the the handler leaves the registers &#40;link register, stack pointer&#41; in tact and unmodified between the interrupt occurring and the ISR entry.
Can the Keil IDE simulate the ADUC devices?&nbsp; If so this should make it simpler and Richard is normally quite good at having a look at your code.
