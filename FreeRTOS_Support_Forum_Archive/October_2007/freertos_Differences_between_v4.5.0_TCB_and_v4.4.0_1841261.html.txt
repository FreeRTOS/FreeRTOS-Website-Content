</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Differences between v4.5.0 TCB and v4.4.0</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on October 9, 2007</div><div class="thread-post-content">There were only a couple of minor changes.
The uxBasePriority member was introduced for the priority inheritance mechanism.&nbsp; This is only used if mutexes are compiled in.
Adding this member could potentially increase the size of the TCB, depending on the configuration, so I optionally allowed another member to be excluded to lessen the impact.&nbsp; The uxTCBNumber member has no use other than debugging/tracing, so I excluded this is the trace facility was not compiled in.
Regards.
