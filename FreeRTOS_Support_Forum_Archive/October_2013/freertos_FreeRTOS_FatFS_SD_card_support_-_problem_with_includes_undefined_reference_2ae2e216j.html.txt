In case the <a href="http://elm-chan.org/fsw/ff/00index_e.html">original libraries</a> were improper for LPC2xxx board, I tried pasting the ones from <a href="http://www.freertos.org/FreeRTOS-Plus/FreeRTOS_Plus_IO/Demo_Applications/LPCXpresso_LPC1769/NXP_LPC1769_Demo_Description.shtml">this project</a> &#8211; no result.
