</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Low Power Modes: When Should vPortSuppressTicksAndSleep (or other) Be Called?</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richarddamon">richarddamon</a> on April 7, 2019</div><div class="thread-post-content">One thing that I notice as that all your defines are preceeded by &#92; which makes them a comment, and not a define, is that how they actually are in FreeRTOSConfig.h ?
Second, you show the configEXPECTED<em>IDLE</em>TIME<em>BEFORE</em>SLEEP 500, but don&#8217;t show your tick rate. If it is less than 1000 Hz, then 500 tick might be longer than 1 Second.
You could put a breakpoint at the test for configEXPECTED<em>IDLE</em>TIME<em>BEFORE</em>SLEEP and see if there is something else running that has a shorted timeout, and that is keeping you from going to tickless idle.
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Low Power Modes: When Should vPortSuppressTicksAndSleep (or other) Be Called?</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/groger57">groger57</a> on April 7, 2019</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hi, thank you for responding Richard.

</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Low Power Modes: When Should vPortSuppressTicksAndSleep (or other) Be Called?</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richarddamon">richarddamon</a> on April 7, 2019</div><div class="thread-post-content">Generally entering (or leaving) low power mode has an energy cost, and you need to expect to sleep long enough that the low power mode will save enough energy to pay for the transistion.
Note, that the time isn&#8217;t how long you wait before going into low power mode, but how long you expect that you will be waiting before going into low power mode (so if you know you are going to be interrupted soon, even though the timeouts are long, you may want to have to pre-check callback prevent the entry to low power mode in that condition.
