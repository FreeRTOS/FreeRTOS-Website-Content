</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Problem changing Tick time from 1ms to 10ms</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on November 21, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">The demo tasks are not required, they are literally just that, there for demonstration so don&#8217;t worry about taking them out.&nbsp;   
Having said that, some actually do some useful tests too, and it is those that are likely to be causing you a problem with a different tick value.  
The prime suspect will be the tasks created by the vCreateBlockTimeTasks&#40;&#41; function call.&nbsp; These test that tasks remain in the Blocked state for the expected number of time.&nbsp; The tests will allow a little margin to account for the fact that interrupts will execute, and other tasks may share their priority.&nbsp; If you have changed the tick frequency it is likely that the measurable time granularity will take the measured block time outside of the allowed margin.  
Maybe the vStartDynamicPriorityTasks&#40;&#41; tasks could suffer a similar problem, but I can&#8217;t remember without looking at their implementation again.  
Regards.
