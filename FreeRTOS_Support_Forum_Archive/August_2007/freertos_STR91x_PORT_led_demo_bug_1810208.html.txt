</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">STR91x PORT led demo bug?</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on August 27, 2007</div><div class="thread-post-content">Either I misunderstand your question or you have some misconceptions about how a multitasking system works.&nbsp; vLEDFlashTask&#40;&#41; is not an ordinary function, but defines a task.&nbsp; The task executes autonomously &#40;by itself&#41;.&nbsp; xTaskCreate&#40;&#41; does not attempt to call vLEDFlashTask&#40;&#41;, it creates the task that is defined by vLEDFlashTask&#40;&#41;.&nbsp; As it is called three times it creates three such tasks, so when the scheduler takes control all three tasks run &#40;pseudo&#41; simultaneously and continuously.
Regards.
