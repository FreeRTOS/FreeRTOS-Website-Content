</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">problems on same priority tasks</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/bkastel1">bkastel1</a> on March 3, 2008</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hello,
maybe it is just a typing mistake but your for&#40;&#41; loop is missing ; &#8211; it should be for&#40;;;&#41;&#123;&#125;. And also you should have a wait condition of somekind &#8211; maybe just a vTaskDelay&#40;some&#95;ticks&#41;; . Otherwise your highest task will not do a context switch to a task of lower priority.

</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">problems on same priority tasks</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on March 3, 2008</div><div class="thread-post-content">The IAR simulator is a core only simulator.&nbsp; This means it does not simulate the timer and therefore the tick interrupt will not be executing.&nbsp; Without the tick interrupt you will never switch tasks unless you use a taskYIELD&#40;&#41; statement &#8211; I&#8217;m not sure if the yielding works in the simulator but I would guess that it did.
