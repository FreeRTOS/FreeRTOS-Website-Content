</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">LM3S6965 Eval RevC OLED did not work</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/djg">djg</a> on September 3, 2008</div><div class="thread-post-content">I have the changes I made to make FreeRTOS 4.7.2 work on a rev C board on my FreeRTOS under Linux page.&nbsp; http://www.pdp8.net/other/freertos&#95;linux/install.shtml
The changes needed in patch &#40;context diff&#41; format are http://www.pdp8.net/other/freertos&#95;linux/rev&#95;c&#95;patch.txt
&#43; is lines added, &#8211; is lines deleted &#33; is lines changed.
