</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">STM32, FreeRTOS and lwIP &#8211; Timeout problems</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on January 7, 2013</div><div class="thread-post-content">You would have to look at the execution pattern of the system to determine what was being slow.&nbsp; For example, does the stack start executing immediately that data is received, or is there a delay because the task processing the data is too low priority.&nbsp; Likewise when anything gets posted on a queue, are the sending task and receiving task running right away or waiting for other higher priority tasks to finish first?&nbsp; Etc.  
I can&#8217;t answer the question as to what the interrupt priority should be without knowing what your configMAX&#95;SYSCALL&#95;INTERRUPT&#95;PRIORITY value is set to in FreeRTOSConfig.h.&nbsp; Basically it needs to be no higher than that &#40;which on a Cortex-M means a numerically lower value&#41;.&nbsp; See the following page: <a href="http://www.freertos.org/RTOS-Cortex-M3-M4.html">http://www.freertos.org/RTOS-Cortex-M3-M4.html</a> and particularly note the requirement to call NVIC&#95;PriorityGroupConfig&#40; NVIC&#95;PriorityGroup&#95;4 &#41;; on STM32 parts.  
Regards.
