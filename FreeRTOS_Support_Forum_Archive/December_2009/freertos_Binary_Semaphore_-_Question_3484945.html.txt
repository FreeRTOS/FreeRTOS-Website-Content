</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Binary Semaphore &#8211; Question</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/dajar">dajar</a> on December 10, 2009</div><div class="thread-post-content">You understand the semaphores correctly, what you are missing is the timing interaction of your two tasks.   
Here is your timing sequence.  
myTask does:   
Take, LED&#95;OFF, Give, Pause  
at the pause mySecTask is allowed to run and the semaphore is available so meSecTask does  
Take, LED&#95;ON, Give, Pause  
So in a very short time &#40;not 1 second&#41; you have had the following sequence  
Take, LED&#95;OFF, Give, Take, LED&#95;ON, Give  
Now both tasks are paused for 1 second, and the cycles repeats.  
If you put an oscilloscope on the LED you will see a short OFF pulse followed by a long ON pulse  
To correct your problem you need to put the Give after the Pause. Do not release the semaphore until your are completely finished with it. In this case you are not finished with the semaphore until the LED blink time is complete.  
Good Luck. And BTW FreeRTOS is awsome.  
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Binary Semaphore &#8211; Question</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richard_damon">richard_damon</a> on December 10, 2009</div><div class="thread-post-content">Even putting the Give after the Wait won&#8217;t always work, for this sort of thing you would need two flags, as here is what would happen &#40;assuming myTask has higher priority:  
MyTask starts, Takes semaphore, LED off, wait<br />
MySecTask starts, Tries to take semaphore but blocks<br />
MyTask wait ends, MyTask gives semaphore, but is still highest task so keeps running &#40;so MySecTask doesn&#8217;t actually take semaphore yet&#41;, MyTask than loops and takes semaphore again and loop continues.
