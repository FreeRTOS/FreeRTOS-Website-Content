</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Problem with timeout</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richard_damon">richard_damon</a> on December 9, 2009</div><div class="thread-post-content">A 1 ms tic rate means delays are only accurate to 1ms, and in fact, the way FreeRTOS works, the time out will expire in no more than the specified time, so a 1ms delay is 0-1ms, a 2 ms delay is 1-2ms, and so on. &#40;The task may not start immediately on that next tick if some higher priority task is running&#41;.  
basically time steps in 1 ms intervals, so by its measure anything in &#8220;tick 10&#8221; is 1 tick before anything in &#8220;tick 11&#8221;, so a 1 tick delay means until the next tick, which might be almost a full tick in time if the interrupt just happended and might be almost no time if the tick interrupt is about ready to happen.
