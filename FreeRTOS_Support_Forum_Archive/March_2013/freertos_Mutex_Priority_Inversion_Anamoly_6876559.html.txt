</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Mutex Priority Inversion Anamoly</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richard_damon">richard_damon</a> on March 2, 2013</div><div class="thread-post-content">Why would you expect the priority 1 or 2 task to every get the semaphore?  
When Task 4 gets the semaphore, everyone else lines up on it and waits for it.<br />
When Task 4 gives the semaphore back, Task3, since it is the highest priority task will get it.<br />
While Task 3 is outputing its message, my guess is that Task 4 finishes its 1 tick wait, and then puts itself in line for the semaphore.<br />
When Task 3 finishes, task 4 get the semaphore because it is the highest priority task waiting on the semaphore.  
Making the semaphore into a mutex just says that when Task 4 starts its wait on the semaphore, Task 3 gets a temporary boost in priority to that of Task 4 &#40;the priority inversion logic&#41; .  
If all tasks are the same priority, then the priority doesn&#8217;t select which task gets chosen, and among equal priority tasks, the one waiting the longest it chosen first.&nbsp;
