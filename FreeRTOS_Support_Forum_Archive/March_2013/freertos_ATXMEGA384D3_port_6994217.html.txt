</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">ATXMEGA384D3 port</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on March 12, 2013</div><div class="thread-post-content">Just from a brief look, without referring to the manuals, I think you need to also be storing RAMX and RAMY &#40;or something like that&#41; in addition to the registers in your post if you want to go above 64K.&nbsp; You would also need to have initial values for the extra registers in the stack setup for a task when the task is created.  
Have a look at the Atmel forum in <a href="http://interactive.freertos.org/">http://interactive.freertos.org</a> to see if you can find an example.&nbsp; You can also ask on <a href="http://www.avrfreaks.net/">http://www.avrfreaks.net</a>  
Regards.
