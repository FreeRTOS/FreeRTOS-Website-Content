<pre><code>--------------------

</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Possible scheduler unfairness?</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on April 10, 2013</div><div class="thread-post-content">Some tasks use their entire time slice, other tasks do something very briefly then yield &#40;be it explicitly by calling taskYIELD&#40;&#41; or by blocking on a queue or semaphore, or even by posting to a queue or semaphore that unblocks a higher priority task resulting in preemption&#41;.  
In this artificial demo task environment, tasks that execute after a task that only used part of a time slice will only ever get part of a time slice themselves.&nbsp; If they start half way through a time slice then they will get swapped out again at the end of the time slice.&nbsp; Real applications do not normally result in this type of execution pattern because real tasks are performing real functionality.
