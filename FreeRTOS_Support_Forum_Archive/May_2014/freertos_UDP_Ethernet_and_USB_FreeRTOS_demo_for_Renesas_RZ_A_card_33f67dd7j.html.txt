</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">UDP/Ethernet  and USB FreeRTOS demo  for Renesas RZ/A card</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/rtel">rtel</a> on May 14, 2014</div><div class="thread-post-content">For USB you could use the drivers provided by Renesas.
For UDP you could use FreeRTOS+UDP (http://www.FreeRTOS.org/udp) with the MAC driver provided by Renesas.
In either case, you would have to build these into your application as (per the previous thread) FreeRTOS is a statically linked system.
Regards.

is FreeRTOS provides a USB driver or demo?
