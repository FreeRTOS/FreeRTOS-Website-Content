</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">problems with priorities</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on September 25, 2007</div><div class="thread-post-content">I don&#8217;t quite follow the sequence here, but it looks like you are not not blocking on your queue send/receives, but instead polling.&nbsp; When the tasks are the same priority this will be inefficient.&nbsp; When the tasks are of different priorities then the low priority task will get starved of processing time and never run.&nbsp; This is because, without blocking, the higher priority task is always wanting processor time.
