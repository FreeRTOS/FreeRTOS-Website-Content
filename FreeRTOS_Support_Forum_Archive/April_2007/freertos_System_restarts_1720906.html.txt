</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">System restarts</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/embeddedc">embeddedc</a> on April 23, 2007</div><div class="thread-post-content">I was going to suggest that you look at the EMAC example as this uses the DMA, but reading your post again it seems you are already doing this.
As you are always passing pdFALSE to portEXIT&#95;SWITCHING&#95;ISR&#40;&#41; then you need not bother with the portENTER&#95;SWITCHING&#95;ISR/portEXIT&#95;SWITCHING&#95;ISR&#40;&#41; macros and could instead just use the &#95;&#95;IRQ attribute &#40;assuming GCC&#41;.&nbsp; This would save you a bit of stack in case it is a stack issue that is causing the problem.
