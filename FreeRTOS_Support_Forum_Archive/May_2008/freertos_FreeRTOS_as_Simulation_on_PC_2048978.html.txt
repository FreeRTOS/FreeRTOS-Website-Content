My simple question is:
&quot;Is it possible to &quot;emulate&quot; FreeRTOS or its API in a pc, running another OS &#40;i.e.: Windows and/or Linux&#41;?&quot;
My goal is developing on a standard x86 PC running Windows/Linux &#40;just to cut down the developing costs and time&#41;, then simply cross-compile all stuff to my embedded board.
Obviously I don&#8217;t want &quot;Real Time Features&quot; in this environment, just all OS-calls just to check the correctness of my software.
Hint: I was thinking about SDL &#40;libSDL&#41; and its thread&#8217;s utilities just to make all stuff cross-platform &#40;from Windows to Linux and vice versa&#41; and hopefully easily enough to debug.
Best regards,
Gianluca
