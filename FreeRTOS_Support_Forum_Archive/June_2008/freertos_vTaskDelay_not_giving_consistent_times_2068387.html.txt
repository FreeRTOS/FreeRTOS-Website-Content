</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">vTaskDelay() not giving consistent times</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/davedoors">davedoors</a> on June 6, 2008</div><div class="thread-post-content">If you just call taskYIELD&#40;&#41; then you are not delaying so FreeRTOS will just choose the same task to run again.
If you call vTaskDelay&#40; 1 &#41; then you are on the limit of the resolution and the period you delay for will depend on where in the current time slice the function is called. This is also very inefficient.
Can you replace regularTask with a tick hook? http://www.freertos.org/a00016.html
