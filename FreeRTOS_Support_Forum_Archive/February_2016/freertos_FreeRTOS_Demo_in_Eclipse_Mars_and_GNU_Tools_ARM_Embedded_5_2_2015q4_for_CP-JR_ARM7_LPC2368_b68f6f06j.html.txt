<em>Resolution from http://stackoverflow.com/questions/20412100/discovery-options-in-eclipse-c-c
Kepler hides &#8220;Discovery Options&#8221; by default. Go to Window->Preferences-> C/C++ > Property Pages Settings and check display discovery option page. Then, open your project preferences and change arm-elf-gcc to arm-none-eabi-gcc like the rest.</em>
