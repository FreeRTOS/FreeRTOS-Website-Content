</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">udata_heap_1.o&#8217; can not fit the section. </h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on June 12, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">Look at the file 18f452.lkr in FreeRTOS/Demo/PIC18&#95;MPLAB.&nbsp; It creates a large data bank called BIG&#95;BLOCK to fit the FreeRTOS heap, you will have to do a similar thing in your linker script.  
Note that there are &#40;documented&#41; problems when a stack allocated from this big block crosses a page boundary, although you will find fixes for this in FreeRTOS Interactive and other locations &#8211; but still the PIC18 is far from an ideal target to run any kind of pre-emptive RTOS because of its paging scheme and hardware stack.
