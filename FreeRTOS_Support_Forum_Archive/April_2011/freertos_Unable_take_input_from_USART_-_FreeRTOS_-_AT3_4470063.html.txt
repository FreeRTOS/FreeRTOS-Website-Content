</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Unable take input from USART &#8211; FreeRTOS &#8211; AT3</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/davedoors">davedoors</a> on April 7, 2011</div><div class="thread-post-content">When you connect the UART to putty, can you see the characters being transmitted from the hardware to putty?
