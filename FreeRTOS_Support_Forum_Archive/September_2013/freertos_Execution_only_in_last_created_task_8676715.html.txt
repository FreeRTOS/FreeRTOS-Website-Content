You also have configUSE&#95;16&#95;BIT&#95;TICKS, which means the maximum number of ticks you can block for is 0xffff.&nbsp; With a tick frequency of 10KHz the tick count will overflow every 6 seconds &#40;which doesn&#8217;t matter to FreeRTOS&#41;.  
When you replace the loop with vTaskDelay&#40;200&#41;; I would expect there to be a minimum of 200 ticks between each increment of a, and I would not expect a to increment more than once before the tick count reached 200 minimum.&nbsp;
