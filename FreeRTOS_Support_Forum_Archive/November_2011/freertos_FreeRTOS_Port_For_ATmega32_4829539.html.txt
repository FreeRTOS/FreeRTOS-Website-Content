</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">FreeRTOS Port For ATmega32 </h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/balace">balace</a> on April 5, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hi dirtycookie, i&#8217;m not an expert on this but i&#8217;ll try to give some tips.  
1&#46; I suggest you remove all unused variables as they require stack, especially those defined by the tasks…e.g ucBit.  
2&#46; Check if your tasks are successfully created.  
3&#46; If the FreeRTOS was correctly implemented, then <em>ToggleLED6off&#40;&#41;</em> task would turn ON the LEDs and vice-versa.  
4&#46; As off now, i think there is no mechanism to determine the exact or actual amount of stack needed by a task…so check if&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; your tasks are being created, if not, then increase the stack size and check again assuming all other things are OK…continue increasing the stack value and checking and you&#8217;ll get an acceptable amount.   
5&#46; <em>configMINIMAL&#95;STACK&#95;SIZE</em> is just a recommended minimum for any task…so do not just rely on this value.   
Just a little observation: second parameter of xTaskCreate&#40;&#41; should be <strong>const signed</strong>  
Check the FreeRTOS manual by Richard Barry.
