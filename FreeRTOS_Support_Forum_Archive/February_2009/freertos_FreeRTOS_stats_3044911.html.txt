</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">FreeRTOS stats</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on February 27, 2009</div><div class="thread-post-content">These functions are actually the most complex part of the whole system as they have to account for every eventuality and possible sequence of events.&nbsp; This is actually where some RTOSes will silently fail to meet their requirements where there are multiple tasks waiting to send or receive and the tasks have different priorities, and interrupts can access the queues &#8211; the scenarios get quite complex &#40;for example, what happens if an interrupt removes an item from a queue after a task has already been ublocked to read the same item?&#41;.&nbsp; The FreeRTOS code attempts to work correctly in all cases &#40;the SafeRTOS testing helps there&#41; while minimising the use of disabling interrupts &#40;again some RTOSes will just disable interrupts for the function, in which case the complexity diminishes&#41;.&nbsp; The function could be made faster by making it longer &#40;more code&#41; and/or greater use of critical sections.&nbsp; For example, you could ignore the possibility of blocking until after recognising that blocking was required, but that would mean performing that whole operation in a critical section.
Regards.
