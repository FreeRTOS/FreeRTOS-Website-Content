1&#46; why is the size in the linkerfile smaller than the configminimalstacksize?<br />
2&#46; when i count, how many bytes we need i get this:  
i think, probably wrong because thousands of people calculated it before me, the calculation is like this:  
3 dummies for debugging<br />
2 for address parameters<br />
1 empty space<br />
16&nbsp; for WREG,status,intcon, bsr, fsr2L,…, pclath<br />
20 for .tmpdata and math&#95;data<br />
2 for top global data section<br />
3 for the address of the task on the stack<br />
1 for number of return addresses<br />
1 for afterwards topofstack++<br />
+&#8211;<br />
49
