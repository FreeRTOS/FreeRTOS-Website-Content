I do not receive a NULL to the semaphore and the task initiates correctly BUT when the line task gets context switched a variable used to store the slope of the line is corrupted.
I´ve made a change to the port for PIC18 as others in the forum tried changing portCOMPILER&#95;MANAGED&#95;MEMORY&#95;SIZE to 0x14 and adding a MOVFF&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; POSTINC0, PREINC1 to the portSAVE&#95;CONTEXT and and a MOVFF&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; POSTDEC1, POSTDEC0 to the portRESTORE&#95;CONTEXT but without success.
