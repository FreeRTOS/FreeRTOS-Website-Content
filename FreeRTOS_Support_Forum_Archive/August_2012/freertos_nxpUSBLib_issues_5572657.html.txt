</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">nxpUSBLib issues</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on August 24, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">USB has very strict timing requirements during the enumeration process, and less strict after that.&nbsp; It might be best to raise the priority of the task and use short delays &#40;or use an interrupt&#41; to enumerate, then lower the priority and use longer delays after that.  
You might also consider using vTaskDelayUntil&#40;&#41; in place of vTaskDelay&#40;&#41; to get less jitter in your timing.
