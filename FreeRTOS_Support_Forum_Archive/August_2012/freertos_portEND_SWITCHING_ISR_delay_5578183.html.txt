I&#8217;m building an application that currently has 4 tasks running<br />
idle with hook, priority 0<br />
two tasks with vTaskDelay in them, priority 1<br />
Communications task with xSemaphoreTake in it, priority 4  
In an interrupt at the end of a DMA I execute<br />
xSemaphoreGiveFromISR&#40;DMA&#95;Done, &amp;xHigherPriorityTaskWoken&#41;;<br />
set bit on a port<br />
portEND&#95;SWITCHING&#95;ISR&#40;xHigherPriorityTaskWoken&#41;;  
the communications task clears the bit as soon as it gets the semaphore  
What I&#8217;m seeing is the communications task doesn&#8217;t run until about the next tick &#40;I haven&#8217;t checked precisely, but it looks to be right after a tick&#41;.&nbsp; I&#8217;ve traced through and xHigherPriorityTaskWoken is true, but when I step out of the interrupt I&#8217;m back in the Idle task.
