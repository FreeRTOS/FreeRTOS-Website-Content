</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">Webserver NXP LPC1768 Cortex M3 Red Suite</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on March 20, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">The first thing to look at would be whether the PHY being used by the FreeRTOS demo is the same as the one on the Keil board.&nbsp; All the hardware specifics are in their own files &#8211; so there should be nothing in FreeRTOS itself, or lwIP outside of its thin hardware port that would make any difference.  
Regarding the change to LPC1758 &#8211; as long as that part has a MAC and adequate RAM, it should be a matter of simply changing the linker script to be correct for that part.&nbsp; If it has less RAM than the LPC1768 then you may need to make further changes to fit the Ethernet buffers in.
