  <main class="main-content"><div class="thread-post"><h2 class="thread-post-title">IAR FreeRTOS stack checking</h2><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/sgardner123">sgardner123</a> on March 8, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content">Hello,  
I am using IAR EWARM 6.30.6 with the FreeRTOS and OpenRTOS plugin &#40;v2.0.0.0&#41; and developing on a TI Stellaris EK-LM3S9D92 board. How do I get the stack checking to turn on. Right now the &#8220;On&#8221; and &#8220;Off&#8221; menu items are dimmed out. Also, in the Tasks window&#8217;s &#8220;Min Free Stack&#8221;, every task is &#8220;Off&#8221;. In my code I have:  
&#35;define configCHECK&#95;FOR&#95;STACK&#95;OVERFLOW 2  
I also noticed that the &#8220;Top of Stack&#8221; is not really the top of the stack for my setup because when single stepping, I see the SP go higher when popping the stack than what is listed in the column &#8220;Top of Stack&#8221;.  
Thanks in advance&#33;
</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">IAR FreeRTOS stack checking</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/richardbarry">richardbarry</a> on March 10, 2012</div><div class="thread-post-content"><blockquote>
  How do I get the stack checking to turn on. Right now the &#8220;On&#8221; and &#8220;Off&#8221; menu items are dimmed out.
</blockquote>
Try right clicking on the column in the table itself.&nbsp; There is a forum for state viewer support on the highintegritysystems.com web site.  
<blockquote>
  I also noticed that the &#8220;Top of Stack&#8221; is not really the top of the stack for my setup because when single stepping, I see the SP go higher when popping the stack than what is listed in the column &#8220;Top of Stack&#8221;.  
</blockquote>
On the ARM the stack grows down from high memory.&nbsp; I would therefore expect to see the start of stack being a lower value than the current top of stack, and the stack pointer to grow away from the start of stack &#40;as functions are entered&#41; value by becoming a larger number, and shrink toward the start of stack value &#40;as functions are exited&#41; by becoming a smaller number.&nbsp; I think that is the behaviour your are reporting, and therefore the expected behaviour.  
Regards.
