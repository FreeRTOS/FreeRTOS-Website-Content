</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">vPortResetPrivilege declared implicitly warning in IAR</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/rtel">rtel</a> on April 10, 2017</div><div class="thread-post-content">The MPU ports have portUSING<em>MPU</em>WRAPPERS set to 1 and

already defined in the GCC versions in the FreeRTOS download.  You will 
need to add them into your IAR version.  For example in the 
FreeRTOS/Source/portable/IAR/[your_port]/portmacro.h.

</div></div><div class="thread-post"><h3 class="thread-post-title">vPortResetPrivilege declared implicitly warning in IAR</h3><div class="thread-post-author">Posted by <a href="http://sourceforge.net/users/rtel">rtel</a> on April 12, 2017</div><div class="thread-post-content">I assumed, given the title of the thread, and the original post, that 
you were trying to use the MPU.  vPortResetPrivilege() and 
xTaskCreateRestricted() are only defined in the MPU port.
Are you just trying to use the normal IAR port for the Cortex-M?  If so, 
then you are simply building the wrong port layer &#8211; you need to build 
the files from FreeRTOS/Source/portable/IAR/ARM<em>CM3 (or ARM</em>CM4F 
depending).  There are lots of example IAR projects in the FreeRTOS 
download for you to use as a reference.
